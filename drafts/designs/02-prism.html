04 / A little more clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

No need to arrive with a technical specification. Start with the work you would like to make easier.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Usually the first step is to assess what you already use. We check available integrations, access and constraints before recommending any changes.

How much does an engagement cost?

Assessment and pilot work are scoped before a quote. Implementation, third-party usage and ongoing support are shown separately, so you can see the full commitment.

Will AI make decisions without our team?

We agree the review and approval boundaries with you. Sensitive actions, uncertain inputs and exceptions can require human approval. Not every task needs AI.

How do you handle access and business data?

Data sources, permissions, providers and retention requirements are reviewed during assessment. The design should use only the access needed for the agreed workflow.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes. Start with one measurable workflow, document what works, and use the results to decide whether a wider rollout is worthwhile.
